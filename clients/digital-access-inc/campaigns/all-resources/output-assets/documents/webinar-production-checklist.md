## PHASE 1 — SETUP (Do these first, before any recording)

### HeyGen Account Setup
- [ ] Sign up at heygen.com (Creator plan ~$29/mo is sufficient)
- [ ] Navigate to **Avatars** → **Create Instant Avatar**
- [ ] Record a 2-minute voice sample (speak naturally — read anything, just needs clean audio)
  - Use a quiet room, good microphone if possible (even AirPods mic works)
  - Speak at your normal pace and tone
  - HeyGen uses this to clone your voice
- [ ] Optional: Record a 1–2 minute front-facing video of yourself for a photo-realistic avatar
  - Face centered, neutral background, good lighting
  - If you skip this, use one of HeyGen's built-in professional avatars

## PHASE 3 — DEMO RECORDING (Screen capture)

### What to Record
A 4–5 minute silent screen recording of the Maven/Growbotik platform. Matt narrates over it in post.

### The Demo Business
**Use:** Roofing contractor, Fort Worth, Texas
- Business name: [Pick any real Fort Worth roofer from Google for authenticity]
- Average job value: $14,200
- Monthly revenue: ~$100K
- Revenue goal: Add $400,000/year

### Recording Steps
- [ ] Open the Maven/Growbotik platform
- [ ] Start screen recording (use Windows Game Bar: Win+G, or OBS, or Loom)
- [ ] Record the intake — answering the 8 questions for the Fort Worth roofer
- [ ] Show the analysis running (progress bar)
- [ ] **CUT** — don't wait for completion, jump cut to completed analysis
- [ ] Slowly scroll through and highlight:
  - Gap scores (all 7 categories)
  - Review count vs. top 3 competitors
  - Top keyword rankings in Fort Worth market
  - Customer acquisition cost comparison
  - The 12-month roadmap (show all 3 budget options)
- [ ] End on the portal view — showing permanent access

### Recording Tips
- Move the mouse slowly and deliberately
- Pause on important numbers for 2–3 seconds
- Don't click around randomly — every movement should be intentional
- 1080p resolution minimum
- No notifications on screen (turn on Do Not Disturb before recording)
