## AD TARGETING RECOMMENDATIONS

**Platform:** Facebook + Instagram
**Audience:** Home Services Business Owners
- Job title: Owner, Founder, President, General Manager
- Industry: Construction, Home Improvement, HVAC, Plumbing
- Interests: Roofing, home renovation, contractor business, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro
- Behaviors: Small business owners
- Geography: Target by metro — start with NC, SC, TX, FL, AZ (storm-prone high-volume states)
- Age: 35–65

**Retargeting Audience:** Website visitors, video viewers (25%+), page engagers

**Budget Recommendation:** Start $50/day per variation. Kill losers at $150 spend. Scale winner to $150–300/day.

**Tracking:** UTM parameters for each variation. Track to email opt-in, not clicks.
