HEADLINE:
7 Marketing Gaps Costing Local Service Businesses
$40,000+ Per Year
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

INTRO (2-3 sentences):
Most local service businesses aren't losing revenue
because of bad marketing. They're losing it because
of specific, measurable gaps that nobody ever bothered
to calculate. Here's what those gaps typically look like
— and what closing each one is projected to deliver.

- - - - -
GAP 1: UNANSWERED CALLS
- - - - -
The gap: Average local service business misses roughly
35-40% of incoming calls during peak hours.

Typical projected impact: At $15,000/job and a 20%
conversion rate, missing 3 calls/day = estimated
$46,500/month in potential revenue walking to competitors.

The fix: AI voice agent that answers every call,
captures the lead, and routes to your phone instantly.

- - - - -
GAP 2: REVIEW DEFICIT
- - - - -
The gap: Top-ranked competitor in most markets has
3-5x the Google review count of the nearest competitor.

Typical projected impact: Review count is a primary
factor in "near me" rankings and conversion rate.
A 78% review gap typically means 2-3 ranking positions
lost — and a meaningfully lower click-through rate.

The fix: Structured review generation program.
Most businesses close the gap within 60-90 days.

- - - - -
GAP 3: PAGE 2 RANKINGS
- - - - -
The gap: Page 1 captures ~75% of search clicks.
Page 2 and beyond captures almost none.

Typical projected impact: Businesses ranking outside
page 1 for their 5 most important keywords are
largely invisible to high-intent buyers searching now.

The fix: Niche-specific, location-specific SEO
targeting the exact searches customers use.

- - - - -
GAP 4: SLOW SPEED-TO-LEAD
- - - - -
The gap: Research shows leads not contacted within
5 minutes are significantly less likely to convert.

Typical projected impact: At $15,000/job, the
difference between answering immediately vs. calling
back 2 hours later is often the difference between
winning and losing the job.

The fix: AI voice agent + automated lead routing.
Captures and responds before the competitor sees
the notification.

- - - - -
GAP 5: WEAK WEBSITE CONVERSION
- - - - -
The gap: Average local service website converts at
1-2%. Optimized sites convert at 4-6%.

Typical projected impact: Doubling conversion rate
on a site driving 200 visitors/month at $15,000/job
could mean 6-12 additional jobs projected annually.

The fix: Mobile click-to-call, visible review widget,
clear value proposition, live chat option.

- - - - -
GAP 6: ZERO AI SEARCH PRESENCE
- - - - -
The gap: Most local businesses have no visibility
on ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity — where an increasing
share of high-intent searches are happening.

Typical projected impact: Early-mover advantage.
The businesses establishing AI search presence now
are positioning for a channel that's growing fast.

The fix: GEO optimization — structured content
and citation strategy for AI search platforms.

- - - - -
GAP 7: NO DATA ON CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST
- - - - -
The gap: Most business owners don't know what it
actually costs to land a new customer — which means
every marketing decision is a guess.

Typical projected impact: Businesses tracking CAC
typically identify 15-30% waste in marketing spend
that can be redirected to higher-performing channels.

The fix: Maven's analysis calculates your estimated
CAC automatically and benchmarks it against competitors.

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
HEADLINE:
The Pre-Sale Checklist: 8 Things Buyers Look For
in Your Marketing and Operations
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

INTRO (3 sentences):
Buyers don't just look at your revenue. They look at
how it's generated, whether it's consistent, and whether
it survives when you leave. Most business owners preparing
to sell haven't gone through this checklist. Here's what
a serious buyer will.

- - - - -
ITEM 1: CONSISTENT, DOCUMENTED INBOUND LEADS
- - - - -
What buyers look for: A marketing system that generates
predictable lead flow — not referrals, luck, or the
owner's personal network.

What most businesses have: Word-of-mouth and occasional
ads with no documented attribution.

What to fix: A structured marketing engine with monthly
reporting showing consistent lead generation over 12+
months.

- - - - -
ITEM 2: AI VOICE COVERAGE (NO MISSED CALLS)
- - - - -
What buyers look for: Evidence that no lead goes uncaptured —
especially after hours.

What most businesses have: Voicemail. Or nothing.

What to fix: AI voice agent that answers every call,
captures lead data, and routes immediately. Documents
every interaction.

- - - - -
ITEM 3: STRONG REVIEW PROFILE
- - - - -
What buyers look for: Review count and rating consistent
with or better than market leaders.

What most businesses have: Review count significantly
below top competitors, often no active generation strategy.

What to fix: Structured review generation over 12 months.
3x current count is a realistic target with a system.

- - - - -
ITEM 4: DOCUMENTED CUSTOMER ACQUISITION COST
- - - - -
What buyers look for: Clear understanding of what it
costs to land a customer — and evidence it's being tracked.

What most businesses have: No idea.

What to fix: Maven calculates your estimated CAC
automatically. Tracking it monthly creates the
documentation buyers want to see.

- - - - -
ITEM 5: REDUCED OWNER-DEPENDENCE
- - - - -
What buyers look for: Evidence the business generates
revenue and operates without the current owner
in every transaction.

What most businesses have: An owner who is deeply
embedded in every major decision, relationship, and
client interaction.

What to fix: Documented intake processes, AI-assisted
customer communication, trained team with clear SOPs.

- - - - -
ITEM 6: AI-INTEGRATED OPERATIONS
- - - - -
What buyers look for: Modern operational infrastructure —
AI in hiring, training, communication, reporting.

What most businesses have: Manual processes.
Often the same ones as 10 years ago.

What to fix: Targeted AI implementation across the
3-4 highest-impact operational areas. Documented
and demonstrated in due diligence.

- - - - -
ITEM 7: DOCUMENTED SOPs FOR EVERY MAJOR PROCESS
- - - - -
What buyers look for: A business that can be learned
and operated by someone other than the founder.

What most businesses have: Process knowledge that
exists only in the owner's head.

What to fix: AI-generated SOPs for hiring, onboarding,
job delivery, customer communication, and reporting.
12-18 months before going to market.

- - - - -
ITEM 8: PERFORMANCE TREND — NOT JUST A SNAPSHOT
- - - - -
What buyers look for: 12-24 months of consistent,
upward-trending revenue with a clear marketing system
behind it.

What most businesses have: A recent good year and
a QuickBooks file.

What to fix: Start now. Every month of documented
improvement is a month of data that strengthens
your position at the negotiating table.
