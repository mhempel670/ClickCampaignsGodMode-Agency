# HeyGen TTS — Matt's Money Rules (final)

**Slides** = exact dollars on screen (`$5,800`, `$468,000`).  
**Speaker notes** = how Matt says it aloud.

## Use these forms

| Amount | Speaker notes say |
|--------|-------------------|
| $5,800/mo | **fifty-eight hundred dollars a month** |
| $18,500 | **eighteen grand** |
| $15,000 | **fifteen thousand dollars** |
| $400,000 | **four hundred thousand dollars** |
| $468,000/yr | **four hundred and sixty-eight thousand dollars a year** |
| $1.2M | **about one point two million dollars a year** |
| $39 offer | **thirty-nine bucks** |

## Do NOT use in notes

- `18K`, `470K`, `six K` (letter K shortcuts)
- `five thousand eight hundred` (robotic)
- `eighteen five hundred` (meaningless)
- `four sixty-eight thousand` (unnatural)

## Grand vs spelled-out

- **grand** for punchy losses: *eighteen grand*
- **fifteen thousand**, **four hundred thousand** — full words for clarity
- **fifty-eight hundred** — OK for $5,800 (how contractors talk)
