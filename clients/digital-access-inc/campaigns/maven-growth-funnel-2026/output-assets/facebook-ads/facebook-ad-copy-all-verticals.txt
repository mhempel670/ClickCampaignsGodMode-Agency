AD TESTING FRAMEWORK
================================================================

PHASE 1 (Week 1-2): Test Primary Text
- Run AD 1A, 1B, 1C against the same headline and creative
- Budget: $30-50/day per ad set
- Winner = lowest cost per webinar registration

PHASE 2 (Week 3-4): Test Headlines
- Take winning Primary Text
- Test 3-4 headline variations
- Budget: $30-50/day

PHASE 3 (Week 5+): Test Creatives
- Take winning Primary Text + Headline
- Test 2-3 different image/video concepts
- Scale winning combination

TARGET METRICS:
- CTR target: >1.5%
- Cost per webinar registration: <$25
- Cost per analysis completion: <$80
- Pause ad sets with CTR <0.8% after 1,000+ impressions
- Scale winners: +20% budget every 3-4 days on positive ROAS

AUDIENCE TARGETING NOTES:
- Home Services: Job title = Owner, President, CEO + Industry targeting
- Exit Mode: Age 50-70, business owners, home services/trades/legal
- PI Attorneys: Job title = Partner, Managing Partner + Legal industry
- Exclude: employees of large corporations, students
- Start with 25-50 mile radius around target markets
- Best launch markets: Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas, Tampa, Jacksonville,
  Raleigh, Phoenix, Denver, Nashville, Orlando
