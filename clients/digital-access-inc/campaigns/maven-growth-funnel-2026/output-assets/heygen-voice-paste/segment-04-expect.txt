When you click the button at the end of this webinar, here's the exact experience you'll have:

========== SCENE 2 | seg04-slide02.png ==========
Step 1: You'll see the same interface you just saw in the demo.
Same look. Same feel. Built for your business.
Step 2: The AI will ask you 8 questions.
Just like you saw with the Fort Worth roofing company.
Answer them as accurately as you can. The more accurate your inputs, the more precise your analysis will be.
Now here's something important:
The AI will provide default estimates for some questions—things like average job value, monthly revenue, typical close rates for your industry.
These defaults are based on industry benchmarks and data from hundreds of businesses like yours.
But you don't have to use the defaults.
If you know your numbers are different—and you probably do—you can type in your own figures.
Your actual average job value. Your actual monthly revenue. Your actual close rate.
The more accurate you are, the better your plan will be.
Because remember: this is your plan. Built for your business. With your numbers.

========== SCENE 3 | seg04-slide03.png ==========
Step 3: The AI starts building your analysis.
This takes about five to seven minutes.
You'll see a progress bar. The AI is working in the background:
Scraping your website
Pulling your competitors' data from Google
Researching 600 plus keywords for your market
Calculating your gap scores
Building your twelve-month roadmap
Now here's the thing:
You don't have to sit there and watch it.
If you need to get back to work, close the window. The analysis will keep running in the background.
When it's done—usually five to seven minutes—you'll get an email with a link to your completed analysis.
Click the link. Log into your portal. Your plan is waiting for you.

========== SCENE 4 | seg04-slide04.png ==========
Step 4: Review your plan.
You'll see:
Your gap scores across all 7 categories
Your current rankings for your top 40 keywords
Your review count vs. your top 3 competitors
Your estimated customer acquisition cost
Your twelve-month growth roadmap with 3 budget options
All customized to your business, your market, your revenue goal.
Step 5: Decide what to do next.
You have three options:
Option 1: Book a fifteen-minute call with our team. No sales pitch. No pressure. Just a walkthrough of your plan and answers to any questions you have about your specific business.
Option 2: Take the plan and run with it yourself. Hand it to your team. Take it to another agency. It's yours. Do whatever you want with it.
Option 3: Work with us to execute it. We built the system that runs this kind of integrated plan faster and more cost-effectively than anyone else. If you want our help, we're here.
But here's what's important:
No matter which option you choose, the plan is yours. Forever.
You have permanent access to your portal. You can log in anytime. Review your analysis. Check your data. Compare yourself to your competitors.
That's the experience.
Simple. Straightforward. No nonsense.
