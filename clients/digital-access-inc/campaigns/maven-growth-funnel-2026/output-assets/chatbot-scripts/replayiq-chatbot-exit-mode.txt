SECTION 1: GENERAL QUESTIONS
================================================================

Q: What is Maven?
A: Maven is an AI-powered marketing and operational
optimization system for business owners who are planning
to sell in the next 1-3 years. We identify the marketing
and revenue gaps that suppress your EBITDA and make
buyers nervous — and build a systematic plan to close
them before you go to market.

Q: Is this a business broker?
A: No. We're not brokers, and we don't provide valuations
or facilitate sales. Think of us as pre-sale preparation —
the work that happens before you engage a broker.
We optimize your marketing, document your operations,
and build the performance track record that supports
a stronger position when you do go to market.

Q: Who is this for?
A: Business owners who are 1-3 years from selling and
want to maximize what they walk away with. Typically
home services, trades, legal, professional services —
any high-ticket service business where marketing
and operational gaps are directly suppressing EBITDA.

Q: Can you tell me what my business is worth?
A: No — that's a business broker or M&A advisor's job,
and a good one is worth their fee. What we do is identify
the marketing and operational gaps that are likely
affecting your EBITDA and buyer perception, and give
you a plan to close them. Different service entirely.

SECTION 2: ABOUT THE ANALYSIS AND EBITDA
================================================================

Q: How does marketing affect my sale price?
A: Buyers apply multiples to EBITDA. Anything that
consistently improves your revenue and reduces your
costs improves EBITDA. Closing marketing gaps —
missed calls, weak rankings, poor conversion — adds
to top-line revenue. Reducing wasted ad spend improves
margins. Better documented revenue is more defensible
to buyers. All of that flows into your numbers over
12-24 months.

Q: What specifically does the analysis show?
A: Seven categories: your Google rankings, review count
vs. top competitors, website conversion score (specific
issues identified), speed-to-lead estimate, AI search
visibility, customer acquisition cost, and a 12-month
optimization roadmap. All framed around what each gap
means for buyer perception and EBITDA, not just growth.

Q: I'm not planning to sell for 2 years. Is it too early?
A: It's actually the right time. It takes 12-18 months
to build the performance trend that means something
to a buyer. If you start now, you walk into a broker
conversation with documented, consistent improvement.
If you wait, you won't have that track record.

Q: What does "documented results" mean?
A: It means we don't just fix the gaps — we generate
a paper trail. Revenue attribution reports, operational
efficiency documentation, AI-generated SOPs for every
major process, analytics dashboards showing consistent
performance. Buyers do due diligence. This is the
package that makes that process fast and clean.

Q: What if my business already has good revenue?
A: Good revenue is a foundation. Documented, consistent,
growing revenue with a marketing system that runs without
you — that's what buyers want to see. The analysis
will show you where there's room to strengthen what
you already have.

SECTION 3: THE THREE PRONGS
================================================================

Q: What's included in the full program?
A: Three areas. First: Revenue optimization — deploying
the full Maven platform to close marketing gaps and
build consistent performance. Second: AI infusion —
modernizing your operations to reduce owner-dependence
and demonstrate scalability. Third: Documentation —
building the SOPs, dashboards, and buyer-ready data room
that makes due diligence easy.

Q: What does AI infusion actually mean?
A: We come in and look at your business processes —
hiring, onboarding, training, customer communication,
job delivery, internal reporting. We identify where
AI can drive efficiency and reduce the need for the
owner to be in the middle of every decision.
Then we implement it and document everything.

Q: Do I need to be tech-savvy for this?
A: No. We handle implementation. You review and approve.
The goal is a business that runs on documented systems —
not that you personally operate the technology.

Q: How long does the program take?
A: We recommend 12-24 months minimum for meaningful
buyer impact. The longer the runway, the stronger
the documented track record. That said, some gaps —
speed-to-lead, review generation — show results faster.

SECTION 4: OBJECTIONS FROM PRE-EXIT OWNERS
================================================================

Q: Why would I invest in marketing if I'm planning to sell?
A: Because the gaps in your marketing are the first
thing a buyer will find. Every gap is a negotiating
point for them. Closing those gaps over the next
12-18 months — and documenting the improvement —
changes that conversation significantly.

Q: Can you guarantee my business will sell for more?
A: No. We don't provide valuations and we don't promise
sale outcomes. What we do is identify and close the
marketing and operational gaps that tend to suppress
EBITDA and reduce buyer confidence. What buyers pay
is between you, your broker, and the buyer.

Q: I've already talked to a broker. Should I still do this?
A: Absolutely — this is exactly what brokers wish their
clients had done before they listed. A documented,
AI-optimized business with 12-18 months of clean
performance data is dramatically easier to sell at
a premium than one without it.

Q: My business is doing well. Do I still need this?
A: Probably. Doing well and being optimized for sale
are two different things. The analysis will show you
specifically what gaps exist, and you can decide
if closing them is worth the investment.

Q: Is my industry right for this?
A: The program works best for service businesses
with high average ticket values — roofing, HVAC,
plumbing, remodeling, legal, healthcare. If you're
not sure, run the analysis. It'll be clear quickly
whether the numbers make sense.
