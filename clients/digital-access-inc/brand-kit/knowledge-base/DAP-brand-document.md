## Section 1: Executive Summary

### 1.1 Company Overview
Digital Access Inc (digitalaccesspro.com) is a fully autonomous digital marketing company serving local service businesses across the United States. Operating under the **Maven** brand — white-labeled from the Growbotics platform — Digital Access Inc delivers AI-driven, continuously optimized marketing programs to high-ticket local service businesses including attorneys, HVAC contractors, roofers, remodelers, plumbers, electricians, pest control operators, insurance agencies, and mortgage brokers.

Digital Access Inc is not a traditional marketing agency. There are no fulfillment teams, no account managers, and no manual reporting. The Maven platform handles all execution, client communication, reporting, billing, and autonomous optimization — under the Digital Access Inc brand — allowing the company to scale to hundreds of clients without adding headcount.

### 1.2 The Platform — Maven by Digital Access Inc
Maven is the white-labeled version of the Growbotics autonomous marketing platform, deployed exclusively under the Digital Access Inc brand. From the client's perspective, Maven is the product — a sophisticated, continuously self-optimizing marketing system. The Growbotics infrastructure is **never disclosed**.

### 1.3 The Business Model

**TIER 1: Front-End — The $39 Maven Custom Marketing Growth Plan**

Every prospect enters through a paid front-end offer: a $39 Custom Maven Marketing Growth Plan. Contains:
- A complete multi-category marketing gap analysis (reviews, SEO, GEO, PPC, near-me, speed-to-lead, website conversion)
- Niche-specific long-tail keywords for their exact service area
- A detailed website conversion scoring report (e.g., 50/100 with specific reasons)
- A precise customer acquisition cost (CAC) estimate
- A full 12-month growth roadmap with monthly service plan, projected leads, and estimated budget
- Three plan options: Fastest to Goal, Most Cost-Effective, and Most Budget-Friendly

**TIER 2: Back-End — 12-Month Maven Marketing Services Execution**

- Sales team commission: ~10% of first month's client revenue
- Markup on wholesale (home services): 70–75%
- Markup on wholesale (legal): 100%
- Projected annual profit per client (75% markup): $30,000–$60,000
- Projected annual profit per client (100% markup): $60,000–$150,000

### 1.4 The Transparent Pricing & Optimization Policy
Core differentiator. Clients are informed upfront:
1. Maven's AI agents continuously monitor and adjust in real time
2. Monthly costs may fluctuate within ~5–10% of the initial quoted price
3. Clients receive advance notice of any recommended pricing adjustments
4. Clients always have the option to opt out of changes
5. Monthly check-in available to all clients
