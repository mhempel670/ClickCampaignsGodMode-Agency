1. **Revenue Stabilization & Optimization** — Close marketing gaps, make revenue predictable and documented

## Brand Voice
- Straight-shooting, dry humor, revenue-obsessed
- For exit clients: add empathy for the transition + financial literacy (EBITDA, multiples, buyer psychology)
- **Sounds like:** A business adviser who's seen the numbers and isn't going to sugarcoat it

**We say:**
- "At $15,000 per roofing job, you don't need to miss many calls before you're staring at serious money."
- "If you're selling in 24 months, closing your marketing gaps now is one of the most direct ways to improve what you walk away with."

**We DON'T say:**
- "Leverage cutting-edge solutions to maximize ROI"
- Anything a marketing agency would say

---

## Language Policy (CRITICAL)
**Approved:** projected, estimated, based on our analysis, our data suggests, EBITDA impact, typical results range from
**Never use:** guarantee, proven, you will get, we promise, your business will sell for, valuation
**Note:** Maven identifies marketing gaps — it does NOT appraise businesses.

## The Three Market Segments

### Home Services
- Roofers, HVAC, Plumbers, Remodelers, Kitchen/Bath, Windows
- Avg ticket: $10K–$50K | Markup: 70-75%
- Growth hook: "At $15,000 per job, even 2-3 missed opportunities a month is $30K–$45K walking to your competitor."
- Exit hook: "You've been roofing for 25 years. The business you've built is worth something."

### Personal Injury Attorneys
- Avg ticket: $50K–$500K+ | Markup: 100%
- Growth hook: "One missed after-hours call = potential $200K case lost."
- Exit hook: "A documented intake system and consistent marketing engine can meaningfully improve what a buyer sees."

### Business Exit (Horizontal — any vertical)
- Owners 50s-70s, 10-30+ years in business, thinking about selling
- Hook: "You're thinking about selling in the next couple of years. Before anything else, you need to know where your marketing and revenue systems are falling short — because that's exactly what buyers will find."
- The math: Flat revenue at 3-4x EBITDA vs. documented AI-driven growth at a meaningfully higher multiple

## 6 Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Show the math before you spend a dime** — gap analysis first, then decide
2. **This is about revenue, not marketing tools** — the number is the north star
3. **The plan keeps improving** — continuous AI optimization + documentation for exit clients
4. **Radical transparency on costs** — 5-10% variance, always notified in advance
5. **Fix speed-to-lead first** — AI voice agent answers every call; for exit clients it's also a buyer asset
6. **Close the gaps that matter to buyers** — EBITDA impact + documentation = stronger position at the table
