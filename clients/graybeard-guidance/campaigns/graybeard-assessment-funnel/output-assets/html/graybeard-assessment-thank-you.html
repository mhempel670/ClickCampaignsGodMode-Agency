From the Desk of Matt Hempel

Thank You—and Welcome.

I'm genuinely glad you're here. Thank you for trusting us with this part of your journey and for giving yourself the time to take a clear-eyed look at what might come next.

I built this because, a few years ago, I found myself at a crossroads too. I knew I still wanted to remain relevant, contribute, participate, and do work that mattered. What I did not know was what that next chapter should look like—or how to get from “there has to be something next” to a direction I could understand and act on. As plans go, staring thoughtfully into the middle distance was not getting much done.

I had decades of experience behind me, but no good way to step back and see what all of it added up to. Short of hiring a very patient professional, spending thousands of dollars, and perhaps revisiting every questionable decision I had made since 1987, there was no obvious place to go. Most of what I found was either an abstract personality test, a collection of esoteric labels, or generic second-career advice that seemed written for someone who had not already lived an entire career. What I wanted was something much more practical: a thoughtful way to uncover the knowledge, judgment, preferences, lessons, and hard-won wisdom that had accumulated over a lifetime—and see what I might actually do with it now.

That's why this Assessment exists. Each of us has lived a singular life. Even when two people have held similar jobs or traveled similar paths, no one else has lived inside your skin, made your decisions, navigated your setbacks, earned your victories, developed your relationships, or learned exactly what you have learned. You bring something to the table that cannot simply be copied or replaced.

At the same time, we are living in extraordinary times. AI and today’s technology give us new ways to draw that experience out, organize it, recognize patterns that may have become invisible to us, and turn what we know into something relevant, useful, and—if that is what we want—marketable. The technology is powerful, but your human experience is the source. Let’s face it, AI has not lived in your skin for the past 30 years, and it cannot manufacture the judgment you spent decades earning.

My hope is that this helps you move from uncertainty and “I know there is something here, but I cannot quite name it” toward clarity, confidence, and a firmer grasp of what you actually carry—and how you might bring it into the world on your own terms.

There is a real person behind all of this. That's me. I'm pleased to welcome you. Take your time, tell the truth, enjoy the conversation, and do not worry about sounding impressive. You have already done the hard part: you lived it.

— Matt Hempel
