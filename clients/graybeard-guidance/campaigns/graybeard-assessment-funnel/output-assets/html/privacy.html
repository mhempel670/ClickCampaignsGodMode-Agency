Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Graybeard Guidance is operated by Digital Access Inc., a North Carolina corporation. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to matt@graybeardguidance.com.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, purchase and subscription details, responses provided during guided conversations, transcripts, call or session identifiers, recording references when available, Assessment and Blueprint materials, support messages, and basic website or device information.

How we use it

We use this information to process purchases, identify the correct participant, connect multiple sessions, prepare and review your Assessment and Blueprint, provide Forum access and support, communicate about your account, prevent fraud, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. If you purchase the Blueprint, approved Assessment context may be securely prepared for the dedicated follow-up conversation after your identity is confirmed.

Service providers and AI processing

We use service providers for hosting, payments, email, databases, authentication, voice-agent conversations, transcription, and AI-assisted document preparation. They may process information only as needed to provide those services. AI assists with organization and drafting; a human approval step is required before a final Assessment or Blueprint is delivered.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store complete card numbers. Your receipt or card statement may identify Digital Access Inc. or Digital Access Pro.

Advertising and analytics

We may use analytics or advertising technologies to understand site activity and measure campaigns. If advertising pixels or nonessential cookies are added, we will use them subject to applicable consent and platform requirements. We do not sell conversation transcripts or completed deliverables.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain participant continuity, resolve disputes, and meet legal or accounting obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet system can be guaranteed completely secure. Transcripts and participant materials are not stored in the public website repository.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. You may unsubscribe from marketing messages and may cancel a Forum subscription at any time by contacting us. To make a request, email matt@graybeardguidance.com.

Children

The service is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service develops. The effective date above will show the latest revision.
