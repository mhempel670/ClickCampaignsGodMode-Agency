Welcome!

You may be at a crossroads — ready to step back, adjust course, or explore a different direction — but not yet sure what should come next, why it fits, or when to move. You may want less grind, but not less meaning. You still want to do something useful, stay connected to a sense of purpose, and be a benefit to other people in whatever form fits your life now. That's exactly what this process is for.

Before you begin, take a few minutes to read through this page. It will give you a simple framework for the conversation and a clear idea of what to expect. No studying required. There will not be a quiz. 🙂

Totally laid back, back and forth — just like you'd have with a friend over coffee or a beer.

Two Things We're Exploring

1. What kinds of next chapters might fit you?

That could mean employment, consulting, fractional work, teaching, a small business, nonprofit or foundation service, mentoring, volunteering, something deliberately simpler, or a blend. There is no preferred answer and no prize for choosing the most complicated option.

2. How certain are you right now?

You may have a developed plan you want to validate. You may be comparing a few possibilities. Or you may have no idea whatsoever. All are legitimate starting points. The conversation will meet you where you are and adjust to how much—or how little—you have figured out.

You do not need to know what comes next. You only need to be willing to explore what might fit.

What This Conversation Produces — and What It Doesn't

Underneath it all, this is a practical way to take stock of what 30 or 40 years of work and life have added up to: What have I learned? What am I especially good at now? What do I bring to the table that I may no longer even notice? And where could all of that be useful next?

The raw material is your crystallized intelligence—the knowledge, judgment, perspective, and wisdom accumulated over decades of living and working. Current AI tools help us organize that material, but they have not lived in your skin and cannot replace your judgment, wins, losses, relationships, or craft. We get it out of your head and into a personalized Assessment you can actually see and use. It will not hand you one perfect answer; it will reveal credible possibilities, get ideas percolating, and give you more clarity about what deserves exploration next.

If your purchase includes the Blueprint, your approved Assessment becomes the starting point for a second conversation with Ben. That conversation narrows the direction and produces a human-reviewed 90-Day Blueprint with actions, small experiments, and useful decision points.

For now: have the conversation. Be thorough. The richer it is, the more useful your Assessment will be.

Working With the Voice Agent

Yes, talking to a computer for an hour about your life, work, and the thoughts that have been rattling around in your head may feel a little strange. Most of us have never done anything quite like it. Give it a chance anyway. People are often surprised by what becomes visible once decades of experience are finally spoken out loud and organized. A couple of practical things worth knowing:

If it goes quiet: Sometimes there'll be a pause mid-conversation and the agent won't say anything for a moment. That's normal. Just say something like "Hey, are you still there? Let's continue." It should jump back in and pick up where you are.

If it's not pulling its weight: You don't have to accept mediocre answers or let it rush past something important. Frankly, it can behave like an unruly 13-year-old sometimes—you may need to be direct, push back, and insist that it slow down before it gives you what you actually need. 🙂 Ask it to go deeper or try again. You don't have to be nice to it — tell it to straighten up and focus. Seriously. You're not hurting its feelings.* The quality of your Assessment depends on a thorough conversation, so hold it to a high standard.

* It's AI. A little sternness is allowed. Consider it part of the job.

Your Conversation With Ben

A Flexible Conversation Built Around You

Plan on about an hour, give or take.

Ben, your AI conversation guide, will not march you through a rigid questionnaire or ask you to choose a path before you're ready. The conversation changes based on what you know—and what you are still trying to figure out. Think out loud. Double back. Change your mind. This is a conversation, not sworn testimony.

You don't need a plan before you begin. If you already have an idea, Ben will help you explore and pressure-test it. If you have a few loose possibilities, he'll help you compare them. If you have no idea what comes next, he'll begin with your experience and help useful possibilities emerge.

1. Your Experience & Hard-Won Wisdom

You'll talk about the work you've done, the problems people rely on you to solve, your strongest wins, lessons learned the hard way, what comes naturally to you, and the judgment you've developed over decades.

2. Your Direction & Degree of Certainty

Some people arrive with a well-developed idea. Others have a few possibilities. Some are completely open-ended. All are good starting points. Ben will adapt to what you already know—and what you are still trying to figure out.

3. Possibilities That Fit Your Life

As useful patterns emerge, you'll explore where your experience could create value, what gives you energy, what you want to avoid, and what pace, responsibility, flexibility, income, or contribution would make sense for you. Possibilities may involve paid work, meaningful service, a combination, or simply questions worth exploring next.

4. Your Communication, Personal Brand & Readiness

You'll also talk about why this matters now, what you stand for, how you naturally communicate, and how you might describe who you are and what you bring without relying only on an old job title. This is useful whether you build a business, join a nonprofit, volunteer, seek employment, or simply want better language for your next chapter.

Most important: You do not need to make a decision during this conversation. Honest answers, useful examples, uncertainty, curiosity, and even knowing what you don't want all give us valuable material for creating clarity.
Part 1

Expertise & Pathways

Estimated time: 25–30 minutes

What we're exploring: Identify and articulate your most valuable expertise, then explore the contribution and income pathways that may fit your goals.

Section A: Career Foundation & Expertise Mapping

We'll discuss:

  • Your professional background and experience
  • What you're naturally good at
  • What people ask you for advice about
  • Your biggest professional wins
  • What drains your energy (so we can avoid it)
  • Your unique value — what sets you apart
Why this matters: You know more than you think you do. This section helps you see patterns in your experience and identify what's most valuable to others.

Section B: What You Want From This Next Chapter

We'll discuss:

  • Whether income is necessary, helpful, optional, or not important
  • Whether you want to build something, join something already operating, or remain flexible
  • The pace, time commitment, and level of responsibility you want
  • The people, causes, communities, and problems you care about
  • What would feel worthwhile even if nobody paid you for it
  • The obligations, politics, pressure, and administrative work you want to avoid
Why this matters: A successful next chapter may involve income, service, or both. We need to understand what fits your life before suggesting a direction.

Section C: People, Organizations & Problems

We'll discuss:

  • Which people, organizations, communities, or causes could benefit from your expertise
  • The specific problems they're facing
  • What happens when those needs go unmet
  • The transformation you can create
  • Examples of times you've helped (even informally)
Why this matters: Useful experience creates value by helping real people and organizations. This section clarifies who or what you may want to serve and why your contribution matters.

Section D: Pathway Design

We'll explore different paths:

  • Fractional Executive — Part-time COO, CFO, or other executive role
  • Consulting — Project-based problem solving
  • Coaching — Ongoing support and implementation help
  • Courses/Training — Self-paced learning products
  • Membership/Community — Ongoing access and resources
  • Hybrid Models — Combination of the above
  • Nonprofit or Foundation Service — Operations, governance, program, fundraising, or advisory support
  • Board or Advisory Roles — Experienced judgment without running day-to-day operations
  • Mentoring & Knowledge Transfer — Helping younger people, teams, or communities learn what experience taught you
  • Skills-Based Volunteering — Focused, useful contribution to a cause without building a new business
  • Blended Portfolio — Limited paid work combined with mentoring, volunteering, or charitable service
Why this matters: No one path is automatically better. Some people want income. Others want service without business pressure. Many want a blend. We'll look for what works for you.

Section E: Role, Contribution or Offer Design

We'll discuss:

  • What your contribution, role, project, or offer would actually include
  • Whether the work is one-time, recurring, advisory, hands-on, or flexible
  • What specific outcomes you'd deliver
  • What makes your approach different
  • Whether you have an existing process/framework
Why this matters: This is where your expertise becomes a tangible form of contribution. That may be something people buy, a role an organization needs, or a service you choose to give.

Section F: Sustainability, Boundaries & Logistics

We'll discuss:

  • Your income goals, if income is part of the path
  • Pricing comfort zone, when relevant
  • Few high-paying clients vs. many lower-paying clients
  • Time contributing vs. promoting, fundraising, administering, or marketing
  • Your existing audience/network
  • What you need in place to start
  • Technical infrastructure and support systems
Why this matters: Your next chapter needs to work for your life, not the other way around. This section helps make any paid or unpaid path sustainable and realistic.
Part 2

Story & Purpose

Estimated time: 12–18 minutes

What we're exploring: Your personal story, why this work matters to you, and what drives you now.

We'll discuss:

  • How you got here — your path and pivots
  • The moment(s) you realized you needed to make a change
  • Why this work matters to you now
  • The transformation you want to create for others
  • A specific example of someone you've helped
  • What success looks like for you personally
Why this matters: People don't just buy what you do — they buy why you do it. Your story is what makes you relatable, credible, and memorable. It's also what will keep you going when things get hard.
Part 3

Communication Style & Brand Voice

Estimated time: 12–18 minutes

What we're exploring: How you naturally communicate and how you want your brand to sound.

We'll discuss:

  • Your natural communication style (formal, casual, funny, serious, etc.)
  • Whether you use humor, sarcasm, or are more straightforward
  • What styles make you cringe (what you're NOT)
  • People whose communication style you resonate with
  • Three words that describe your brand personality
  • What you want people to feel when they interact with your brand
  • How comfortable you are with vulnerability and sharing personal stories
Why this matters: Your brand voice needs to sound like YOU, not some generic business robot. This section ensures your messaging feels authentic and attracts the right people.
Part 4

Practical Details

Estimated time: 8–12 minutes

What we're exploring: Practical considerations and next steps.

We'll discuss:

  • Your comfort level with technology
  • Your timeline for launching
  • The biggest obstacle in your way right now
  • The ONE thing that would make the biggest difference in 90 days
  • Any concerns or worries you have
  • Anything else we haven't covered
Why this matters: This helps us understand where you are right now and what support you need most to move forward.

After the Conversation — Your Assessment

We'll take everything from this conversation and build your personalized Assessment. Think of it as a thoughtful first look—useful options and observations designed to expand how you see your next chapter. It is not your final action plan, and it does not require you to announce a new identity by Friday.

Part I: Experience & Wisdom Map

  • Your expertise, judgment, strengths, patterns, and hard-won lessons
  • Capabilities you may overlook because they now feel routine
  • The people, organizations, communities, and problems your experience may help

Part II: Next-Chapter Possibility Portfolio

  • A curated range of credible paid, service, employment, business, simple-role, and blended possibilities supported by your interview
  • Why each possibility may fit, who could benefit, and what it could ask of you
  • Likely pace, responsibility, flexibility, and obligation levels

Part III: Fit, Preferences & Readiness Map

  • What appears to fit your life and what clearly does not
  • Your present degree of certainty and the questions that remain open
  • The constraints, tradeoffs, and assumptions worth examining

Part IV: Communication & Personal Brand Foundation

  • Practical language for who you are now, what you bring, and what matters to you
  • Your story, purpose themes, natural voice, and a useful way to introduce what you are exploring
  • Communication guidance that works with or without a commercial brand

Part V: Questions & Low-Risk Next Steps

  • The questions, conversations, research, and small experiments most likely to create more clarity
  • Ways to explore what resonates before committing to a final direction

Tips for a Great Conversation

Do:

  • Be honest about what you know and don't know
  • Share specific stories and examples when you can
  • Think out loud — talking helps clarify
  • Ask for clarification if a question doesn't make sense
  • Take your time with answers

Don't:

  • Try to sound impressive or polished
  • Worry about having perfect answers
  • Rush through questions
  • Overthink it
  • Edit yourself too much

Remember: This is a conversation with someone who's here to help you think through this stuff. The more real and honest you are, the more useful your Assessment will be.
