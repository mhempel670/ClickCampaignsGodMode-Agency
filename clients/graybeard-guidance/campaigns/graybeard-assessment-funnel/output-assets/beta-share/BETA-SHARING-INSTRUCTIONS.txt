WHY NOT USE THE PROSPECTCONNECT LINK?
-------------------------------------
Links like funnel.prospectconnect.ai/...?preview=true are hosted on Centripe's
platform account. Visitors see Centripe branding and free-trial prompts — not
your clean buyer experience. This beta-share folder is Centripe-free.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------
  index.html      — Simple menu (starts with delivery page)
  delivery.html   — Conversation guide + voice agent (what testers need now)

No Centripe URLs. The voice agent loads from voiceagents.tech (required for
the AI conversation). Google Fonts load from the internet (page still works
without them, just plainer text).


================================================================================
OPTION A — RECOMMENDED: NETLIFY DROP (free, 2 minutes)
================================================================================

Best for: Sharing a real https:// link. Voice agent + microphone work.

Steps:
  1. Open this folder on your computer:
       clients\graybeard-guidance\campaigns\graybeard-assessment-funnel\
       output-assets\beta-share

  2. In your browser, go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop

  3. Drag the entire "beta-share" FOLDER onto the page (not individual files).

  4. Netlify gives you a random URL, e.g.:
       https://random-name-12345.netlify.app

  5. Send testers THIS link (or add /delivery.html to skip the menu):
       https://random-name-12345.netlify.app/delivery.html

  6. Optional: In Netlify, click "Site settings" → "Change site name" to get
     something readable like graybeard-beta-test.netlify.app

To update later: drag the folder again (same Netlify site) or replace files
in the Netlify dashboard.

No account required for a quick drop (you can sign up free to keep the same URL).


================================================================================
OPTION B — FALLBACK: ZIP AND EMAIL
================================================================================

Best for: Quick offline peek at the written guide only.

Steps:
  1. Right-click the "beta-share" folder → Send to → Compressed (zipped) folder
  2. Email the .zip to your tester
  3. Tell them to unzip and double-click index.html or delivery.html

WHAT WORKS from a zip (opening files on their computer):
  ✓ Reading the conversation guide text
  ✓ Page layout and styling (mostly)

WHAT DOES NOT WORK reliably from a zip (file:// on their computer):
  ✗ Microphone / voice agent — browsers block mic on local files for security
  ✗ Voice agent panel may show blank or error
  ✗ Some browsers block the voice widget iframe on local files

Bottom line: Zip is OK to preview the guide copy. For the full voice
conversation test, use Option A (Netlify) so testers have an https:// link.

ADDING THE SALES PAGE LATER
================================================================================

When you want testers to see the full funnel:
  1. Copy graybeard-assessment-sales-page.html from the html\ folder into
     beta-share\ and rename to sales-page.html
  2. Copy the images\ folder (hero.png, mastery.png, brotherhood.png) into
     beta-share\images\
  3. In sales-page.html, change image paths from ../images/ to images/
  4. Update index.html to link to sales-page.html
  5. Re-deploy to Netlify (drag folder again)

Checkout and payment pages stay on your live funnel host — not needed for
delivery-page beta testing.
